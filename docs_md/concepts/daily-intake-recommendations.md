## Key Ratios to Watch

**Sodium to Potassium ratio (Na:K)**
- Recommended: ≤0.6 mg/mg
- Current US average: 3,400 mg Na / 2,500 mg K = ratio of 1.36 (far too high)
- This ratio is a better predictor of hypertension than sodium or potassium intake alone
- Suboptimal but acceptable goal: ratio of 1 (if ≤0.6 is not achievable)
