# Dairy Products

---

## Milk

- **Pasteurization note**: Regular pasteurization does not significantly change nutrient content. Ultra-high-temperature (UHT) pasteurization (135–150°C) does affect many vitamins.
- **If boiling**: Some lactose converts to lactulose (non-digestible). Some long-chain fats convert to short- and medium-chain fats (may support weight loss and gut health). B vitamins decrease slightly — but milk is not a primary source of B vitamins except riboflavin (B2), which is rarely deficient.
- **Tip**: Heat milk slowly over medium heat and stir while bringing to a boil.

**Key nutrients** (per 100ml):

| Nutrient | Amount | % Daily Value |
|---------|--------|---------------|
| Vitamin B2 | 0.173 mg | ~16% |
| Vitamin B5 | 0.336 mg | ~7% |
| Vitamin B12 | 0.38 mcg | ~16% |

## Greek Yogurt

- **Why eat**: Good protein content, live cultures (probiotics), convenient source of B2 and selenium.

**Key nutrients** (per 100g):

| Nutrient | Amount | % Daily Value |
|---------|--------|---------------|
| Vitamin A | 90 mcg | ~13% |
| Vitamin B2 | 0.233 mg | ~21% |
| Vitamin B5 | 0.47 mg | ~9% |
| Selenium | 12.4 mcg | ~23% |

## Kefir

- **Why eat**: Fermented milk drink; contains probiotics and live cultures. Generally better tolerated than milk in people with lactose intolerance (bacteria partially digest lactose). Supports gut microbiome diversity.
- **Key nutrients**: protein, calcium, B12, B2, phosphorus, probiotics
