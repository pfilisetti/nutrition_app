# Nuts and Seeds

Nuts and seeds are energy-dense foods rich in healthy fats, protein, fiber, and micronutrients. Most omega-3 in plant sources is ALA — the body converts it inefficiently (~5%) to EPA/DHA. Pair plant omega-3 sources with direct EPA/DHA sources (fatty fish).

---

## Almonds

- **Why eat**: Top plant source of vitamin E; very high in biotin, magnesium, and healthy fats. Can be eaten as whole nuts or almond butter (equivalent nutrient profile).

**Key nutrients** (per 28–30g):

| Nutrient | Amount | % Daily Value |
|---------|--------|---------------|
| Vitamin E | 6.8 mg | ~45% |
| Vitamin B2 | 0.21 mg | ~19% |
| Vitamin B3 | 1.12 mg | ~8% |
| Vitamin B7 (biotin) | 19.3 mcg | ~64% |
| Vitamin B9 (folate) | 38 mcg | ~10% |
| Fiber | 3.7g | ~15% |
| Magnesium | 70 mg | ~23% |
| Omega-6 | 3.7g | — |

**Breakfast note**: dark chocolate + almonds = ~61% vitamin E; almonds alone = ~64% biotin

---

## Walnuts

- **Why eat**: Highest plant-based ALA omega-3 content among common nuts; also contains polyphenols.
- **Omega-3**: High in ALA (plant-based) — conversion to EPA/DHA is inefficient; complement with fatty fish.
- **Key nutrients**: ALA omega-3, polyphenols, magnesium, vitamin E, fiber

---

## Chia Seeds

- **Why eat**: One of the richest plant sources of ALA omega-3 and fiber; also good iron and magnesium.
- **Omega-3 type**: ALA — conversion to EPA/DHA is inefficient (~5%). Pair with fatty fish.

**Key nutrients** (per 28–30g):

| Nutrient | Amount | % Daily Value |
|---------|--------|---------------|
| Vitamin B1 | 0.18 mg | ~16% |
| Vitamin B3 | 2.5 mg | ~18% |
| Omega-3 (ALA) | 5,050 mg | — |
| Fiber | 9.8g (85–93% insoluble) | ~39% |
| Iron | 2.2 mg | ~12% |
| Magnesium | 96 mg | ~31% |
| Selenium | 15.8 mcg | ~29% |

## Flaxseeds (Graines de Lin)

- **Why eat**: Rich in ALA omega-3 and fiber; good B1 and magnesium source. Grind before eating for better nutrient absorption (whole seeds may pass undigested).
- **Omega-3 type**: ALA

**Key nutrients** (per 10g / 1 tbsp):

| Nutrient | Amount | % Daily Value |
|---------|--------|---------------|
| Vitamin B1 | 0.17 mg | ~15% |
| Vitamin B7 (biotin) | 3.4 mcg | ~11% |
| Omega-3 (ALA) | 2,350 mg | — |
| Fiber | 2.8g (66% insoluble) | ~11% |
| Magnesium | 40 mg | ~13% |

## Dark Chocolate (80%+)

- Technically a seed-derived food (cacao beans). Often combined with nuts for a nutrient-dense breakfast or snack.

**Key nutrients** (per 25g):

| Nutrient | Amount | % Daily Value |
|---------|--------|---------------|
| Vitamin E | 2.4 mg | ~16% |
| Vitamin B1 | 0.11 mg | ~10% |
| Fiber | 2.5g (90% insoluble) | ~10% |
| Iron | 3 mg | ~17% |
| Magnesium | 57.5 mg | ~19% |

**Breakfast note**: dark chocolate + almonds = ~61% vitamin E; dark chocolate + chia + almonds + oats = ~16g fiber, ~72% magnesium, ~8.2 mg iron
