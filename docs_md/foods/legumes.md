# Legumes and Plant Proteins

Legumes are excellent sources of plant-based protein, fiber, iron, and folate. They contain antinutrients (phytic acid, lectins) that can be reduced by soaking, sprouting, or cooking. For iron absorption: pair with vitamin C; avoid eating with coffee or tea.

---

## Lentils

- **Why eat**: One of the best plant sources of iron and fiber; also high in protein and folate.
- **Antinutrients**: Phytic acid (0.4–1.5% dry weight) — reduce by soaking and rinsing before cooking.

**Key nutrients** (per 100g cooked):

| Nutrient | Amount | % Daily Value |
|---------|--------|---------------|
| Fiber | 10.7g | ~43% |
| Iron | 3.3 mg | ~18% |

---

## Chickpeas

- **Why eat**: Versatile legume; good fiber and iron source. Iron content drops significantly when using canned (1 mg vs 2.89 mg from dry).
- **Tip**: Cook from dry for best iron content.

**Key nutrients** (per 100g):

| Nutrient | Amount | % Daily Value |
|---------|--------|---------------|
| Iron | 2.89 mg (from dry) / 1 mg (canned) | ~16% / ~6% |
| Fiber | 7.6g (per 100g hummus) | ~30% |

## Kidney Beans

- **Safety**: Raw kidney beans contain high concentrations of lectin (phytohemagglutinin) — must be soaked for at least 5 hours and boiled in fresh water for at least 30 minutes before eating.

**Key nutrients**:

| Nutrient | Amount | % Daily Value |
|---------|--------|---------------|
| Fiber | 7.4g (per 100g) | ~30% |
| Iron | 2.6 mg (per 90g) | ~14% |

---

## Tofu

- **Why eat**: Good plant-based protein and iron source; versatile in cooking.
- **Antinutrients**: Contains phytic acid (0.1–2.9% dry weight) — reduces iron and zinc absorption from the same meal.

**Key nutrients** (per 100g):

| Nutrient | Amount | % Daily Value |
|---------|--------|---------------|
| Iron | 2.66 mg | ~15% |

---

## Quinoa

- **Type**: Pseudocereal (seed, not a true cereal) — consumed like a grain but technically a legume-adjacent plant protein
- **Why eat**: One of the few plant foods that provides complete protein (all 9 essential amino acids). High in antioxidants (quercetin, kaempferol) and magnesium.

**Key nutrients** (per 100g cooked):

| Nutrient | Amount | % Daily Value |
|---------|--------|---------------|
| Vitamin B1 | 0.1 mg | ~9% |
| Vitamin B9 (folate) | 42 mcg | ~10% |
| Fiber | 3g | ~12% |
| Magnesium | 64 mg | ~21% |
